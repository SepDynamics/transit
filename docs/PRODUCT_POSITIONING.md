# SENTINEL: Turning Transit Data Into Operational Decisions

Sentinel converts existing transit data into ranked, explainable operating
context. It starts with GTFS and GTFS-Realtime, then expands with
agency-authorized telemetry, ridership, asset, weather, signal, and event data
to support faster, more defensible operational decisions.

It is designed to begin with existing agency feeds and scale from a corridor
pilot to network-wide decision support. Sentinel does not replace CAD/AVL,
dispatch, maintenance, or signal-control systems; it is the evidence and
decision-support layer connecting their data to prioritized, auditable,
advisory recommendations.

## Capability boundary

- **Available now:** GTFS/GTFS-RT reliability intelligence: routes, vehicles,
  delays, headways, alerts, feed quality, ranked evidence, replay, and public
  or protected views.
- **Next:** event-aware risk and forecasting after historical demand, travel,
  and outcome data meets validation criteria.
- **Agency-integrated:** maintenance, fleet, TSP, safety, and equity analyses
  only after their dedicated data-access, governance, and pilot contracts are
  accepted.

Cost savings and ROI are measured pilot outcomes, never guarantees. TSP
recommendations are candidates for agency review, not direct signal control.
